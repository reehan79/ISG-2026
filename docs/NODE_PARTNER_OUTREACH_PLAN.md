# Node Partner Outreach Plan

**Status:** CURRENT OUTREACH SOURCE OF TRUTH  
**Updated:** 2026-09-23

This document supersedes the narrower 12 September outreach plan preserved in history.

## Objective

Build a credible MissionLab Network around the ISG 2026 international campaign and carry the same institutional nodes into the later MissionLab Challenge and remote mission programme.

## Participation classes

### 1. Mission Hub — IST / SSTRL
Central Pakistan coordination, Ground Support/Mission Control, experiment integration and node-network coordination.

### 2. Operational Mission Nodes — selected Pakistani universities
Small number of technically serious universities with defined specialist roles, named faculty ownership and rehearsal/live participation.

The ask is technical participation first, not an ISG fee.

### 3. Participant Mission Nodes — broader schools/universities
Institutions that enter the MissionLab Challenge may activate early, obtain MissionLab access and join selected ISG-linked distributed mission activities before progressing into the Challenge.

Their later Challenge registration fee is for the MissionLab Challenge package, not official ISG participation.

### 4. National Technical / Strategic Node
Suitable for SUPARCO or similar organizations when formally agreed.

Ask for a bounded technical/advisory contribution, focal person and continuity into the later national programme. Financial support may be discussed separately; it is not a node-registration requirement.

### 5. International Collaborator Node
Suitable for institutions such as KFUPM or SQU when formally agreed.

Use for controlled distributed MissionLab activity and future institutional collaboration. Do not label them Pakistan Ground-Lab nodes without organizer authorization.

## Outreach order

A practical sequence is:

- secure/confirm the small operational-node core;
- approach the national technical/strategic organization;
- open broader Challenge-linked participant-node onboarding;
- add selected international collaborator nodes;
- develop sponsor packages around the now-visible programme/network.

## What every active node should have

- a named institutional/faculty owner;
- defined node class;
- approved role and permissions;
- campaign enrollment;
- onboarding/connectivity check;
- clear communication channel;
- path into the later MissionLab activity.

## What not to do

- do not collect an "ISG registration fee";
- do not imply official ISG status for Challenge registrants;
- do not invite many decorative logo-only operational nodes;
- do not make an external node a scientific dependency;
- do not promise hardware/HIL access that is not actually available.

## Long-term value

The node network should become a real multi-institution reference deployment for MissionLab / Digital Twin / TwinLink and a pathway into permanent institutional use after the Challenge.
