# Student Recruitment, Selection and Media Activation

**Status:** CANONICAL PROCESS  
**Updated:** 2026-09-24

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the programme-level process for identifying, recruiting, activating and assigning IST students for the Pakistan-side ISG 2026 / MissionLab Network activity.

It governs:

- student recruitment from earlier ISG expression-of-interest and assessment pools;
- recruitment from the later IST–ISG Mission Network role-interest pool;
- direct volunteer/referral recruitment;
- ground-side mission and mission-communications activation;
- provisional role assignment;
- short evidence-based trial tasks;
- final role confirmation and recognition.

It does **not** contain private student data.

## 3. Conflict and precedence rule

Use the following rule when information differs:

1. this repository defines the **process and allowed claims**;
2. the newest relevant private Drive source defines the **current student-level fact**;
3. an explicit later programme decision must be recorded in this repository before it becomes the new canonical process;
4. Moscow travel/readiness status must not be inferred from old EOI or interview data when a newer Moscow-readiness source exists.

Do not reconstruct the student pipeline from chat memory alone.

## 4. Privacy boundary

The ISG-2026 repository is public.

Never commit:

- student phone numbers;
- personal email addresses;
- registration numbers where they are not already intended for public release;
- passport or visa information;
- consent documents;
- private assessment notes;
- private shortlist spreadsheets;
- parent/guardian information;
- raw form exports.

The private candidate/communications tracker belongs in Google Drive.

## 5. Recruitment evidence model

Recruitment should use cumulative evidence rather than one interview or one form.

Useful evidence includes:

- **EOI signal:** student originally volunteered for ISG;
- **continued-engagement signal:** student responded to Phase-2 or assessment scheduling;
- **skill signal:** student declared media, communication, documentation, software, technical, leadership or event skills relevant to the task;
- **Mission Network signal:** student explicitly opted into the wider IST ground-side activity;
- **direct-volunteer signal:** student personally volunteered or was referred and then actively showed up;
- **delivery signal:** student completes a small assigned mission task and submits evidence.

The last item — actual delivery — should carry the most practical weight for confirming responsibility.

## 6. Moscow readiness is a deployment flag, not a merit ranking

The Moscow final-readiness pool is a separate deployment lane.

A student in that pool may still contribute before travel or serve as a Moscow–IST liaison, but a person who may deploy to Moscow should not be the sole owner of a critical IST ground-side task.

The ground-side structure must remain operational even if the Moscow deployment list changes.

## 7. Recruitment stages

### Stage R0 — Evidence pool

Any student appearing in an earlier EOI/Phase-2/assessment/Mission Network source, or any new direct volunteer, may enter the evidence pool.

### Stage R1 — Media / Mission Activation Candidate

A student is invited to a short activation task.

This is not a permanent title and does not guarantee a certificate or leadership role.

### Stage R2 — 72-hour Awareness Sprint

Use a short, visible, measurable task instead of another interview round.

All candidates receive one approved ISG/IST message package and must complete a small assignment within the sprint window.

Minimum common task:

- place the approved mission visual/message on WhatsApp Status or another agreed channel;
- distribute it to at least one relevant class/batch group;
- distribute it to at least one appropriate society/club/department channel where available;
- provide simple evidence of completion.

Candidates may also receive one specialist task according to their skills.

### Stage R3 — Confirmed Mission Communications / Department Role

After the sprint, assign roles based on actual delivery, reliability, quality and ability to coordinate with others.

### Stage R4 — Ongoing Mission Network role

Roles continue through pre-ISG preparation, the 26 October–1 November mission period, and the wider December activity only while the participant remains active and useful.

Recognition is contribution-based.

## 8. Recommended provisional clusters

Before the sprint, use functional clusters rather than inflated leadership titles.

### A. Campus Amplification

Purpose: department/class/batch/society reach.

Typical provisional label:

**Media Activation Candidate — Campus Amplification**

### B. Content & Visual

Purpose: photography, video, reels, graphics, captions and authentic behind-the-scenes content.

Typical provisional label:

**Media Activation Candidate — Content & Visual**

### C. Digital Amplification

Purpose: web/social posting support, digital assets, dashboards, link distribution and campaign evidence.

Typical provisional label:

**Media Activation Candidate — Digital Amplification**

### D. Documentation & Evidence

Purpose: activity logs, photo/video evidence, captions, participant stories and mission archive.

Typical provisional label:

**Media Activation Candidate — Documentation & Evidence**

### E. Technical Storytelling

Purpose: translate experiments, Ground Mission, MissionLab, Digital Twin/TwinLink and student technical work into accurate, understandable content.

Typical provisional label:

**Media Activation Candidate — Technical Storytelling**

## 9. Roles that may be confirmed after demonstrated contribution

Use these only after evidence of contribution:

- **Mission Communications Associate**
- **Department Mission Ambassador**
- **Content & Visual Associate**
- **Digital Amplification Associate**
- **Documentation & Evidence Associate**
- **Technical Storytelling Associate**
- **Moscow–IST Media Liaison**
- **Mission Communications Coordinator** — use sparingly for actual owners/coordinators.

Do not call every participant a coordinator.

## 10. Department Mission Ambassador principle

The first campus-awareness objective is broad IST penetration.

A Department Mission Ambassador is responsible for ensuring that the approved mission message reaches multiple relevant batches/classes/societies in that department, not merely the student's friends.

Department coverage should be monitored across:

- Aerospace / Aeronautics & Astronautics;
- Avionics;
- Electrical Engineering;
- Mechanical Engineering;
- Materials Science and Engineering;
- Space Science;
- Computing / Computer Science / AI;
- other relevant IST academic groups.

## 11. Media is a secondary duty across the Mission Network

Mission communications is not limited to the media team.

Every active Mission Network member should support approved communication in some form, for example:

- carry an approved message to a relevant student group;
- document a technical activity;
- provide a short technical explanation;
- support a photograph/video;
- help a department ambassador reach students;
- repost approved official content.

The dedicated communications team controls message consistency and production quality.

## 12. 72-hour Awareness Sprint target

Initial campus target:

**Within approximately three days, students across the major IST departments should have encountered the ISG / IST Ground Mission message at least once.**

Recommended evidence:

- distribution screenshots;
- list of class/batch/society channels reached;
- one short reel/video or photo pack from content candidates;
- one short technical explainer from technical-story candidates;
- simple tracker status: invited / accepted / submitted / reviewed / confirmed.

Do not ask students to invent independent claims or unofficial posters. Use the approved message house and claims rules.

## 13. Evaluation after the sprint

Do not use another long interview unless a specific leadership role genuinely requires it.

Review only practical evidence:

1. did the student respond;
2. did the student complete the task;
3. was the output usable and accurate;
4. did the student submit evidence on time;
5. did the student help the wider team rather than only self-promote.

Use this evidence to assign or withhold role titles.

## 14. Public-message boundary

All student-produced or student-distributed content must follow:

- `docs/communications/MESSAGE_HOUSE.md`
- `docs/master/AUTHORITY_AND_CLAIMS.md`

The central story is IST/Pakistan participation, ground-side mission operations, authentic student technical activity, distributed MissionLab engagement, the two experiments, and continuity toward the larger MissionLab programme.

Do not imply that a local student, school or university has official ISG participant status unless that status is actually confirmed.

## 15. Required workflow for future threads / agents

For any student recruitment, selection, ground-team or media-activation request:

1. read the canonical programme documents;
2. read this document;
3. query the live private Google Drive sources listed in Section 2;
4. identify the latest Moscow-readiness state separately;
5. identify the latest Mission Network / recruitment tracker state;
6. only then propose or change student assignments;
7. update the private tracker for operational changes;
8. update this repository only when the **process, role taxonomy or policy** changes.

This prevents student selection from drifting across chats, forms and ad-hoc messages.
