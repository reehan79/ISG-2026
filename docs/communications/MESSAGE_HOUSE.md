# MissionLab Network 2026 — Message House

**Status:** CANONICAL COMMUNICATIONS FRAME  
**Updated:** 2026-09-23

## Core one-sentence narrative

**MissionLab Network 2026 is a distributed mission-operations and STEM programme connecting the ISG 2026 international campaign with the IST Ground Support activity, participating university/school Mission Nodes, the later MissionLab Challenge and a progression toward remote KidSat/Digital Twin missions.**

## Core story

ISG provides the international mission context and credibility.

The distributed Mission Node network gives Pakistani institutions and selected international collaborators a real technical participation pathway.

The MissionLab Challenge converts that early exposure into a structured, fee-bearing engineering programme.

Remote KidSat/TwinLink missions extend the same platform from simulation toward physical/hybrid experimentation.

## Audience framing

### Universities / schools
Join or activate a Mission Node, obtain early MissionLab exposure around the ISG campaign, and progress into the MissionLab Challenge.

### National technical organizations
Join as a bounded National Technical / Strategic Node and contribute specialist capability, advice or validation without becoming a scientific dependency.

### International universities
Join as an International Collaborator Node for controlled distributed MissionLab activity and a pathway to later institutional teaching/evaluator use.

### Sponsors
Support a national programme that links an international space event, distributed university/school participation, a national engineering challenge and later remote hardware access.

### Media
Focus on Pakistan/IST participation, student mission operations, distributed national technical engagement, the two experiments and the continuity into a larger national MissionLab activity.

## Required wording discipline

Say:
- "ISG-linked"
- "distributed MissionLab Node"
- "IST Ground Support / Mission Control"
- "MissionLab Challenge registration"
- "remote KidSat / TwinLink mission" when actually available

Do not say without evidence:
- "official ISG participant" for a school/university simply because it joined MissionLab;
- "ISG registration included";
- "flight-qualified";
- "Mars-qualified hardware";
- "AI-controlled Mars mission";
- "live HIL" unless a validated physical resource is actually in the loop.

## Brand relationship

The strongest positioning is:

**IST/SSTRL — mission/programme hub**  
**CubeSTEM MissionLab — enabling platform/technology**

The scientific result should remain scientifically framed. Product credibility should come from real use and evidence.
