# Institutional Participation Models

**Status:** CANONICAL  
**Updated:** 2026-09-23

## 1. IST / SSTRL

Role: **Mission Hub**

Primary value:
- Pakistan Ground Support / Mission Control;
- programme integration;
- organizer-facing coordination;
- experiment and evidence coordination;
- node-network leadership.

## 2. Selected Pakistani universities

Role: **Operational Mission Node**

Typical ask:
- one faculty focal person and backup;
- a small committed student/research team;
- defined technical role;
- connectivity test and rehearsal;
- participation during agreed ISG-linked windows;
- continuity into the MissionLab Challenge / later programme.

No ISG fee is charged for this role. Challenge teams may later register separately under the Challenge rules.

## 3. Schools and broader university participants

Role: **Participant Mission Node**

Primary pathway:
- register for the later MissionLab Challenge;
- receive early node/team activation and MissionLab access;
- complete orientation/training missions;
- join selected ISG-linked distributed activities;
- continue into the paid Challenge;
- potentially continue into remote KidSat missions.

The Challenge fee pays for the Challenge programme/package, not official ISG participation.

## 4. National technical organizations

Role: **National Technical / Strategic Node**

Suitable for organizations such as SUPARCO when formally agreed.

Possible contribution:
- communications / ground-segment expertise;
- telemetry analysis;
- evidence validation;
- technical observer/advisor;
- mission-scenario input;
- jury/technical continuity into the later Challenge;
- programme support where separately approved.

Do not make such an organization a scientific single point of failure.

Financial support may be discussed separately from node status.

## 5. International universities / organizations

Role: **International Collaborator Node**

Suitable for institutions such as KFUPM or SQU when formally invited/confirmed.

Possible participation:
- parallel engineering analysis;
- controlled mission tasks;
- distributed Digital Twin exercise;
- evaluator/teaching collaboration;
- later institutional MissionLab deployment.

Do not represent them as part of the Pakistan national Ground Laboratory without organizer approval.

## 6. Sponsors

A sponsor is a funding/support relationship, not automatically a Mission Node.

Sponsor support may be directed toward:
- Ground Support operations;
- student engagement;
- connectivity/cloud;
- prizes;
- equipment;
- outreach/media;
- later national Challenge activity;
- travel/logistics where institutionally appropriate.

## 7. Participation principle

Institution-first and mission-first.

The strongest long-term CubeSTEM value comes from real multi-institution use of the platform, not from forcing product branding into the scientific experiment.
