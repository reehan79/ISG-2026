# Node Partner Outreach Plan

**Status:** SOURCE OF TRUTH FOR EXTERNAL NODE OUTREACH  
**Updated:** 2026-09-12

## 1. Objective

Build a small, credible Pakistan technical-node network around IST for the ISG 2026 Ground-Lab activity.

The first outreach is **not a sponsorship request**.

The ask is:

> Join an IST-led Pakistan MissionLab / Ground-Lab activity as a defined technical node linked to the International Space Games 2026, contribute a small specialist team, participate in rehearsals / live activity, and continue into the later national mission activity.

This creates real technical value now and a stronger future reference for distributed Digital Twin / MissionLab deployment.

## 2. Recommended node structure

### Tier 1 — IST / SSTRL / NCGSA

Role:
- Pakistan national hub;
- organizer-facing interface;
- Pakistan Mission Control / Ground Lab;
- experiment integration and evidence archive;
- node coordination.

### Tier 2 — Operational university nodes

October target: **two universities**.

Each operational node should provide:

- one named faculty focal person;
- one backup;
- 3–5 committed students / researchers;
- one defined technical role;
- reliable Internet / workstation access;
- participation in a connectivity test and full rehearsal;
- availability during the agreed ISG operating window.

Candidate technical roles:
- communications / RF / SDR / link analysis;
- control systems / ROS / autonomy;
- telemetry / fault analysis;
- independent evidence validation;
- systems engineering / mission planning.

### Tier 3 — National technical node

Preferred first approach: **SUPARCO**.

Suggested role:
- technical observer / strategic national partner;
- independent telemetry or communications analysis;
- evidence-validation / ground-segment advisory role;
- participation in selected Ground-Lab / media / technical sessions.

Do not make SUPARCO a critical dependency.

### Tier 4 — Associate / observer nodes

Additional universities may be invited later for:
- read-only telemetry / released datasets;
- parallel analysis;
- technical sessions;
- December follow-on participation.

## 3. Who to approach first

Recommended first wave:

1. **SUPARCO** — strategic national technical node.
2. **NUST** — strong controls / robotics / communications / systems capability.
3. **Air University** — aerospace / communications / controls alignment.
4. **COMSATS Islamabad** — software / AI / networking / distributed systems capability.
5. **PIEAS or GIKI** — strong engineering depth, depending faculty responsiveness.

Do not invite too many operational nodes initially. Two strong university nodes are better than six weak logos.

## 4. What to ask universities

The university invitation should be framed around technical collaboration and student participation, not money.

Core ask:

- nominate one faculty focal person;
- nominate 3–5 students / researchers;
- choose or accept one technical-node role;
- join one connectivity test and one rehearsal;
- operate their node during the ISG Ground-Lab activity;
- participate in the later larger national mission activity.

What they receive:

- participation in an international ISG-linked national activity;
- access to a structured Digital Twin / mission-operations exercise;
- student exposure to mission operations, telemetry and fault analysis;
- visibility as a Pakistan technical node;
- first-mover position for the later national activity;
- potential future technical collaboration with IST/SSTRL.

## 5. University outreach message — first contact

**Subject / WhatsApp heading:** Invitation to join IST-led Pakistan Ground-Lab Node for International Space Games 2026

Dear Prof. [Name],

IST / SSTRL is coordinating Pakistan's participation in the International Space Games 2026 and is establishing a Pakistan Ground Laboratory / Mission Control activity linked with the international event.

We are planning a small distributed network of selected university technical nodes, with IST serving as the national hub. Each node will have a defined technical role — for example communications/SDR analysis, control systems, telemetry/fault analysis, mission systems or independent technical verification — and will participate in the live Ground-Lab activity and associated rehearsals.

We would be very pleased if [University] could join as one of the technical nodes. At this stage we are not seeking financial support; we are looking for a committed faculty focal person and a small student/research team who can take ownership of a defined technical role.

The same network is intended to continue into a larger national mission activity later in the year, so we see this as the start of a practical inter-university technical collaboration rather than a one-day event.

If this is of interest, I can share a one-page concept and discuss which technical role would best fit your team.

Best regards,  
Dr. Rehan Mahmood  
Associate Professor, IST / Director SSTRL

## 6. What to ask SUPARCO

Do **not** lead with a sponsorship request.

First ask SUPARCO for:
- participation as a national technical / strategic node;
- nomination of a technical focal person;
- bounded contribution in telemetry, communications, ground-segment operations or evidence validation;
- participation / representation in the IST Ground-Lab activity;
- possible technical observer / jury / advisory continuity into the later national activity.

Why this is better:
- it creates substantive national-agency participation;
- it makes the ISG activity more credible;
- it avoids making the first contact look like a funding request;
- once the role is accepted, a later sponsorship / logistics / technical-support conversation becomes much easier and more defensible.

## 7. SUPARCO first-contact message

**Subject / WhatsApp heading:** Invitation to participate as National Technical Node — Pakistan ISG 2026 Ground Laboratory

Dear [Name],

IST has been coordinating Pakistan's participation in the International Space Games 2026 and is establishing the Pakistan Ground Laboratory / Mission Control activity at IST.

In addition to the central IST node, we are forming a small distributed technical network and would be honoured to involve SUPARCO in a bounded national technical role — for example independent telemetry / communications analysis, ground-segment technical support, or evidence validation during the ISG-linked activity.

The intention is to keep IST as the national coordinating hub while bringing selected universities and national technical organizations into clearly defined specialist roles. The same network can then continue into a larger national mission activity later in the year.

At this stage, our primary request is technical participation and nomination of a suitable focal person rather than financial support. Once the activity structure is finalized, we can separately discuss any other form of support if considered appropriate.

I would be happy to share the short concept note and proposed node roles.

Best regards,  
Dr. Rehan Mahmood  
Associate Professor, IST / Director SSTRL

## 8. Sponsorship — later, not first contact

Potential later sponsorship targets may include:

- SUPARCO;
- Ignite / MoITT ecosystem;
- PARS / relevant national science organizations;
- telecom operators;
- banks / technology companies;
- aerospace / defence technology companies.

But sponsor outreach should begin only after we can show:

- confirmed IST Ground-Lab structure;
- at least two university nodes;
- submitted/accepted Model Experiment(s);
- event window and media plan;
- clear sponsor properties and measurable visibility.

The sponsor pitch should then fund a defined national activity, Ground-Lab capability, student engagement, connectivity, media or later national mission programme — not merely international travel.

## 9. Commercial / long-term value

The node network is valuable for future CubeSTEM MissionLab / Digital Twin adoption because it demonstrates:

- multi-institution deployment;
- common experiment/scenario execution;
- distributed telemetry / evidence;
- faculty/student usability;
- repeatable node onboarding;
- a reference architecture beyond a single IST laboratory.

However, the external invitation should remain **institution-first and mission-first**. Product discussions can follow after the nodes have experienced the platform and the technical value is visible.
