# ISG -> MissionLab Challenge -> Remote KidSat Continuity

**Status:** CANONICAL PROGRAMME JOURNEY  
**Updated:** 2026-09-23

## 1. Commercial principle

ISG is the international activation and credibility milestone.

The paid product/programme is the later MissionLab Challenge and subsequent MissionLab services, not access to ISG itself.

## 2. Early registration model

MissionLab Challenge registration may open before ISG.

A registered team may receive, as part of its Challenge package:

1. institutional Mission Node activation;
2. team/participant accounts;
3. MissionLab orientation;
4. one or more Digital Twin training missions;
5. selected ISG-linked distributed node activity;
6. post-ISG engineering missions;
7. December Challenge participation;
8. remote KidSat/TwinLink access where included in the final Challenge design.

This creates a continuous learning/engineering journey rather than a one-day competition.

## 3. Claim boundary

Early Challenge registration does not create official ISG entrant status.

External language must use "ISG-linked" or equivalent unless official organizer recognition supports a stronger claim.

## 4. Mission readiness

The programme may use a Mission Ready gate before the Challenge, for example:

- node activated;
- participants onboarded;
- Digital Twin familiarization completed;
- one telemetry/fault exercise completed;
- one ISG-linked distributed mission activity completed where scheduled.

Exact gates remain configurable and should not be described as organizer requirements unless they are.

## 5. December progression

After ISG, the same nodes/accounts progress into the MissionLab Challenge.

Challenge missions may include communications, EPS, ADCS, telemetry/fault analysis, mission planning and evidence/reporting.

## 6. Physical progression

Where available, a later mission may bind to a remote KidSat/TwinLink resource.

The educational/scientific mission identity should remain stable while the provider changes from simulation to physical/hybrid.

## 7. Long-term conversion

The desired institutional pathway is:

```text
ISG-linked exposure
  -> active Mission Node
  -> MissionLab Challenge
  -> remote KidSat / HIL
  -> permanent institutional MissionLab use
```

This is the central continuity model for development, marketing and partner outreach.
