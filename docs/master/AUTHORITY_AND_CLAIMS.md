# Authority, Claims and Public-Boundary Rules

**Status:** CANONICAL  
**Updated:** 2026-09-23

## 1. Authority hierarchy

For programme decisions, use this order:

1. explicit current decisions in this repository;
2. frozen scientific experiment documents for MARS-LINK / TWIN-RESILIENCE;
3. current implementation truth in the appropriate technical repository;
4. historical decision records for context only.

History explains why decisions were made. It does not override a newer explicit decision.

## 2. Institutional and product positioning

- IST/SSTRL is the Pakistan mission/programme hub for this activity.
- CubeSTEM MissionLab may be identified as the enabling technology/platform contributor where factually appropriate.
- The scientific experiments are not to be described as product-validation exercises.
- External organizations must not be described as confirmed partners/nodes until that status is actually confirmed.

## 3. ISG participation claim boundary

Do not imply that payment to the MissionLab Challenge purchases official ISG participation.

Safe formulations include:

- "ISG-2026-linked distributed mission activity"
- "MissionLab Node participating during the ISG 2026 international campaign"
- "IST Ground Support / MissionLab activity linked with ISG 2026"

Avoid phrases such as "official ISG participant", "ISG registration included", or equivalent unless directly supported by organizer confirmation for that institution/team.

## 4. Scientific independence

MARS-LINK and TWIN-RESILIENCE must remain scientifically completable locally/offline in Moscow.

Distributed nodes, media links, Pakistan connectivity and later HIL are additive. They must not become required for scientific completion.

## 5. International-node boundary

Foreign institutions may be described as International Collaborator Nodes when formally involved.

They must not be described as part of the Pakistan national Ground Laboratory unless organizer authorization supports that claim.

## 6. Fee boundary

- ISG-linked participation under this programme: no fee.
- MissionLab Challenge: separate fee-bearing programme.
- Early platform/node access may be part of a Challenge registration package.
- Exact fee/pricing is not frozen in this repository yet.

## 7. Technology claim boundary

Do not claim flight qualification, Mars qualification, autonomous Mars control, validated flight AI, or physical/HIL evidence where only simulation or software evidence exists.

Do not relabel simulated/derived evidence as measured physical telemetry.

## 8. Public repository hygiene

This repository is currently public. Do not commit:

- personal identifiers or student personal data;
- passports, visas, travel-document details or private consent data;
- credentials, secrets, tokens or passwords;
- private phone numbers/emails unless already intended for public release;
- confidential sponsor negotiations;
- unpublished pricing or commercially sensitive deal terms;
- private correspondence that was not intended for publication.

Decision history should be a structured summary of programme reasoning, not a verbatim dump of private chat or correspondence.
